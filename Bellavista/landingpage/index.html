ABOUT US

Bella Vista Hotel & Events Place is a premier eco-chic boutique hotel and events destination nestled at the scenic foothills of the mountains. It offers a tranquil, elegant, and exclusive retreat for families, tourists, and corporate clients looking for a peaceful sanctuary away from the hustle and bustle of the city

Established in 2021, Bella Vista began as an exclusive family-owned resthouse. Due to growing demand for intimate celebrations and restful getaways, it was expanded and transformed into a boutique hotel and events venue. Today, it is celebrated for its warm Filipino hospitality, picturesque views, and top-tier service for both accommodations and special events.
