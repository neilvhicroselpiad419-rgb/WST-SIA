TERMS AND POLICIES

Payment & Reservation
Policies

Downpayment & Balance:

A downpayment of 50% is required to secure and confirm any reservation. The remaining balance must be settled in full before or upon check-in.

Incidental Deposit:

A refundable security deposit (e.g., ₱2,000 per room / ₱5,000 for the Event Hall) is required upon arrival to cover potential incidentals, room charges, or damages.

Accepted Payment Methods:

The hotel accepts cash, major credit cards, and verified bank/e-wallet transfers.

Occupancy & Child
Policies

Strict Capacity
Enforcement:

Guest counts are strictly enforced. Exceeding maximum room or Event Hall capacity will result in denied entry or mandatory additional room bookings.

Child Policy & Discounts:

Children 7 and under stay free when sharing existing beds (counts toward max physical capacity). Children aged 8 to 12 receive a 40% student discount. Guests 13 and older are charged standard adult rates.

Unregistered Visitors:

Non-staying guests are restricted to public areas and must vacate the premises before 10:00 PM.

House Rules & Code of
Conduct

No Smoking Policy:

Smoking is strictly prohibited inside all guest rooms and enclosed facilities. A fine of ₱2,000 will be automatically charged for violations. Designated outdoor smoking areas are provided.

Quiet Hours:

Noise and loud activities must be kept to a minimum after 10:00 PM around the accommodation areas to ensure the peace and comfort of all guests.

Damage & Loss:

Guests shall be held liable and billed for any damage or loss caused to hotel property, linens, furniture, or fixtures during their stay.

Pet Policy:

Pets are welcome, but owners are strictly responsible for cleaning up after them. Failure to do so will result in a penalty fee of ₱1,000

Outside Food & Beverage:

Bringing outside catering or large quantities of food into the resort is subject to management approval and standard corkage fees.

Eco-Chic &
Sustainability
Guidelines

Energy Conservation:

Guests are strictly encouraged to turn off air conditioning, lights, and water heaters when leaving their rooms.

Single-Use Plastics:

In line with the hotel's eco-friendly mission, single-use plastics (like balloons or confetti for events) are discouraged on the property.

Event Venue Specific
Rules

Decorations:

All event decorations must be freestanding. Nailing, tacking, or taping items to the Event Hall walls, wood fixtures, or ceilings is strictly prohibited.

Supplier Ingress/Egress:

Third-party suppliers (caterers, florists, sounds & lights) must strictly follow the approved setup and teardown schedule to avoid disrupting hotel guests.
